About

Performer. Storyteller. Singer.

Alex Fish is a singer and actor with his sights set on the Broadway stage. From musical theatre to screen work, he brings energy, warmth and craft to every role.

Alex Fish is the kind of performer who walks into a room and the room immediately sits up straighter.

Part singer, part actor, and entirely unstoppable, Alex has been chasing the spotlight since he could hold a note, and the spotlight has finally stopped running. His voice has been described as “suspiciously good,” “illegally smooth,” and “the reason my neighbors now own a karaoke machine.”

On stage, he brings big heart, bigger energy, and a smile that reaches the back row of the balcony and then goes upstairs to say hi to the lighting booth. Comedy, drama, a ballad that makes you cry into your playbill, or a showstopper that makes you forget you have a phone: Alex does it all, and he does it with style.

His goal? Broadway. His method? Showing up, working harder than everyone else, and hitting every high note like it owes him money.

Catch him now, before he’s too famous to answer your emails. (He’ll still answer them. He’s very nice.)
